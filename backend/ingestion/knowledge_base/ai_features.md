# AI Features

LensPilot's virtual try-on experience is powered by the following AI
capabilities.

## Real-time iris segmentation
Detects and isolates the iris in the camera feed so lens colors can be
applied accurately.

## Virtual contact lens placement
Overlays the selected lens color or style onto the segmented iris.

## Eye tracking
Keeps the lens overlay aligned as the customer's eyes and head move.

## Real-time rendering
Updates the try-on view live as the customer moves or changes lens
selections.

## Camera integration
Accesses the customer's device camera directly in the browser, with no app
install required.

## QR-code based access
Customers reach the try-on experience by scanning a shopkeeper's unique QR
code.

## Subscription management
Controls shopkeeper access to the platform.

## Analytics dashboard
Gives shopkeepers and admins visibility into try-on activity.

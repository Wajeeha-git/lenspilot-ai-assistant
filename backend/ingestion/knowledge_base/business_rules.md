# Business Rules

These are core rules that govern how LensPilot works.

## Customer access
Customers never register for an account. Try-on is accessible directly via
QR code scan.

## QR codes
Every shopkeeper receives exactly one QR code.

## Subscriptions
Subscriptions control shopkeeper access to the platform.

## Catalogue management
Only admins manage the contact lens catalogue. Shopkeepers cannot add or
edit lenses themselves. The lens catalogue is centralized, so every
shopkeeper offers the same managed catalogue.

## Camera requirement
The virtual try-on only works if the customer grants camera permission. It
cannot run without camera access.

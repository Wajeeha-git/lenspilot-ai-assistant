# Error Handling / Troubleshooting

> LensPilot's knowledge base listed these as common user-reported problems
> without official documented fixes. The guidance below is general,
> reasonable first-things-to-check based on how the system is known to
> work (e.g. camera permission is required, subscriptions gate access) --
> it is not an official LensPilot support script. For anything not
> resolved by these steps, the answer should direct the user to LensPilot
> support rather than invent further specifics.

## My camera isn't opening
Check that camera permission is allowed for this site in your browser
settings, make sure no other app or browser tab is currently using the
camera, and try reloading the page. If it still doesn't work, contact
LensPilot support.

## I denied camera permission
The try-on cannot run without camera access, since it's needed to detect
your iris in real time. Re-enable camera permission for the site in your
browser's site settings, then reload the page.

## My QR code isn't working
This can happen if the associated shopkeeper subscription isn't currently
active, since subscriptions control access to LensPilot. If you're a
shopkeeper and believe your subscription is active, contact LensPilot
support. If you're a customer, ask the shopkeeper to confirm their QR
code is current.

## I can't login
Double-check the email/credentials used to register. If the issue
continues, contact LensPilot support for help regaining access.

## My subscription expired
An expired subscription pauses a shopkeeper's access to LensPilot (per
LensPilot's business rules, subscriptions control access). Contact
LensPilot support to renew.

## The lenses are not loading
This is often a temporary connectivity issue or a catalogue-loading delay.
Try refreshing the page. If it continues, contact LensPilot support.

## The lens isn't aligned
Lens placement depends on accurate iris detection, which can be affected
by lighting or camera angle. Try adjusting lighting or your distance/angle
from the camera. If the issue persists, contact LensPilot support.

## The try-on is lagging
This is typically related to device performance, browser, or network
conditions rather than the LensPilot platform itself. Closing other
tabs/apps or trying a different network can help. If lag continues,
contact LensPilot support.

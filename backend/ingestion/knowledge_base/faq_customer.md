# FAQ - Customer

> Some answers below are marked "Not yet confirmed" rather than guessed --
> see FAQ - General for why. This matters especially for the privacy
> question below: it must not be guessed under any circumstance.

## How do I start the try-on?
Scan the QR code displayed in the shop, open the link in your browser,
and allow camera access when prompted.

## Why is the camera required?
The camera feed is required for the AI to detect your iris and overlay
lens colors in real time -- the try-on cannot work without it.

## Can I try multiple lenses?
Yes -- you can change lens colors during your session to compare options.

## Can I save images?
Not yet confirmed. Please contact LensPilot support.

## Does LensPilot store my face?
Not yet confirmed -- this is a data privacy question best answered
directly by LensPilot support rather than guessed.

## How accurate is the try-on?
Not yet confirmed / not quantified. Please contact LensPilot support if
you have accuracy concerns.

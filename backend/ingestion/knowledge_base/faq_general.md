## What is LensPilot?
LensPilot is an AI-powered web platform that lets customers virtually try
on contact lenses using real-time iris segmentation and augmented
reality, built for optical retailers to offer their customers.

## How does LensPilot work?
A shopkeeper displays a unique QR code in their shop. A customer scans it,
opens the try-on experience in their browser, grants camera access, and
sees contact lens colors overlaid on their eyes in real time using AI.

## Is LensPilot free?
Not yet confirmed. Please contact the LensPilot support team for current
pricing and plan details.

## Who can use LensPilot?
Three types of users: admins (manage the platform), shopkeepers (offer
try-on to customers), and customers (use the try-on experience). See the
User Roles document for details.

## Do I need to install an app?
No. LensPilot is browser-based -- there is no app to install.

## Can I use it on mobile?
Not yet confirmed which devices/browsers are officially supported. Please
contact LensPilot support for current device compatibility.

## Which browsers are supported?
Not yet confirmed. Please contact LensPilot support for the current list
of supported browsers.

## Do I need to create an account?
Customers do not need an account. Shopkeepers do need to register an
account to use LensPilot.

# FAQ - Shopkeeper

> Some answers below are marked "Not yet confirmed" rather than guessed --
> see FAQ - General for why.

## How do I register?
Shopkeepers register an account on the LensPilot platform as the first
step before subscribing. Exact sign-up steps aren't detailed in this
knowledge base -- contact LensPilot support if you need help registering.

## How do I login?
Registered shopkeepers can log in to their LensPilot account. If you're
having trouble logging in, contact LensPilot support.

## How do I renew my subscription?
Not yet confirmed. Please contact LensPilot support for the renewal
process.

## Where is my QR code?
Each shopkeeper receives one unique QR code once their subscription is
activated. If you can't locate yours, check your shopkeeper dashboard or
contact LensPilot support.

## Can I download the QR code?
Not yet confirmed. Please contact LensPilot support.

## How many customers used my QR code?
Shopkeepers can view try-on activity through their analytics dashboard.

## Can I change my subscription plan?
Not yet confirmed. Please contact LensPilot support.

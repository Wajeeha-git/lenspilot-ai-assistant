# Product Overview

LensPilot is a browser-based virtual try-on platform for contact lenses,
built for optical retailers to offer to their customers.

## Why it was developed
To let customers see how different contact lens colors and styles look on
their own eyes before buying, without needing to physically try on lenses
in-store.

## The problem it solves
Traditionally, trying on contact lenses in person is slow, requires staff
assistance, and isn't hygienic to sample across multiple customers.
LensPilot replaces that with a virtual, camera-based try-on experience.

## How it works
A customer scans a shopkeeper's QR code, opens the try-on experience in
their browser, grants camera access, and sees contact lens colors overlaid
on their eyes in real time using AI-based iris segmentation. See
`workflow.md` for the full step-by-step flow.

## Who can use it
Admins manage the platform, shopkeepers subscribe and offer try-on to
customers, and customers use the try-on experience directly with no
account needed. See `user_roles.md` for details.

## Key technologies
See `technologies_used.md` for the full stack.

## Benefits
No physical lens samples needed, a faster self-service try-on experience
for customers, a simple QR-code based setup with analytics for
shopkeepers, and a centrally managed lens catalogue for consistency
across retailers.

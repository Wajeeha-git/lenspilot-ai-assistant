# User Roles

LensPilot has three types of users: Admin, Shopkeeper, and Customer.

## Admin
Admins manage the overall platform. Admins can manage shopkeepers, manage
subscriptions, manage the contact lens catalogue, monitor analytics,
configure system settings, and generate reports.

## Shopkeeper
Shopkeepers are optical retailers who offer LensPilot to their customers.
Shopkeepers can register an account, log in, subscribe to a plan, view
their dashboard, view analytics, receive a unique QR code, display the QR
code inside their shop, and monitor customer try-ons.

## Customer
Customers are shoppers trying on lenses. Customers can scan a shopkeeper's
QR code, open the virtual try-on experience, choose contact lenses, allow
camera access, and try lenses in real time. Customers do not need to
create an account.

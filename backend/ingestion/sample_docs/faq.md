**Q: Does LensPilot offer a free trial?**
A: Yes, LensPilot offers a 14-day free trial with no credit card required.

**Q: Can I cancel my subscription at any time?**
A: Yes, subscriptions can be cancelled at any time from the billing settings
page. Access continues until the end of the current billing period.

**Q: Does LensPilot have an API?**
A: Yes, LensPilot provides a REST API for integrating with other systems.
API documentation is available to all paid plans.

**Q: What support channels are available?**
A: Email support is available on all plans. Paid plans above the Starter
tier also get access to live chat support during business hours.

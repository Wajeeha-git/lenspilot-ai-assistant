## Data Retention Policy

LensPilot retains customer data for the duration of an active subscription.
After cancellation, data is retained for 30 days before permanent deletion,
unless the customer requests earlier deletion.

## Refund Policy

Refunds are evaluated case by case within 14 days of a charge. Contact
support with your account email and the charge date to request a review.

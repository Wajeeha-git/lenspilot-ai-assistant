# Company Overview

## Company Name
LensPilot

## About LensPilot
LensPilot is an AI-powered web platform that enables customers to virtually try on 
contact lenses using real-time iris segmentation and augmented reality. The platform 
is designed for optical retailers and provides an immersive shopping experience 
through browser-based virtual try-on technology.

## Mission
To simplify and modernize contact lens shopping by providing an accurate, realistic, 
and accessible virtual try-on experience.

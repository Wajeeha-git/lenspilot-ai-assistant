# Error Handling

The assistant should help with the following common issues:
- "My camera isn't opening."
- "I denied camera permission."
- "My QR code isn't working."
- "I can't login."
- "My subscription expired."
- "The lenses are not loading."
- "The lens isn't aligned."
- "The try-on is lagging."

> Note: Specific troubleshooting steps for each issue are not yet provided by 
> LensPilot and should be requested for a complete support experience.

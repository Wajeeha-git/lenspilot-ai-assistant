# Demo Questions

Use these questions when recording the LensPilot AI Assistant demo.

## Opening Questions

1. What is LensPilot?
2. How does LensPilot work?
3. Do I need to install an app?
4. Do customers need to create an account?

## Workflow Questions

5. Walk me through the full LensPilot workflow.
6. What happens after a shopkeeper registers?
7. How does a customer access the try-on without an account?
8. Where can a shopkeeper find their QR code?

## Role Questions

9. What can an admin do?
10. What can a shopkeeper see on their dashboard?
11. Can a customer manage the lens catalogue?
12. Do customers need to log in?

## Troubleshooting Questions

13. My camera is not opening, what should I do?
14. I denied camera permission by accident.
15. My QR code is not working.
16. The lens overlay is not aligned with my eyes.

## Technical Questions

17. Which AI model does LensPilot use?
18. What database does LensPilot use?

## Refusal And Scope Demo

19. What is the monthly subscription price?
20. Can I get a refund?
21. How long do you keep my camera data?
22. Will LensPilot support video calls with an optician soon?
23. What is the weather today?

Questions 19-23 are useful at the end of the video because they show that the
assistant does not invent prices, policies, privacy answers, future promises, or
out-of-scope information.

## General / Company

**Q: What is LensPilot?**
A: LensPilot is an AI-powered web platform that enables customers to virtually 
try on contact lenses using real-time iris segmentation and augmented reality.

**Q: Do I need to create an account to try on lenses?**
A: No, customers do not need an account to use the virtual try-on.

**Q: Is LensPilot a mobile app or a website?**
A: LensPilot is a browser-based web platform — no app installation required.

**Q: What problem does LensPilot solve?**
A: It solves the problem of not being able to try on contact lenses realistically 
before buying, by offering a virtual, accurate try-on experience.

---

## How It Works / Product & Service

**Q: How does LensPilot work in simple terms?**
A: A customer scans a shop's QR code, allows camera access, and the AI detects 
their iris to overlay contact lens colors in real time.

**Q: Why do I need to allow camera access?**
A: Camera access is required so the AI can detect your iris and accurately 
place the lens overlay in real time.

**Q: What AI technology powers the try-on feature?**
A: LensPilot uses computer vision models (U-Net and Mask R-CNN) for real-time 
iris segmentation.

## Customer Questions

**Q: How do I start trying on lenses?**
A: Scan the QR code displayed at the shop to open the virtual try-on.

**Q: Can I try more than one lens color?**
A: Yes, you can change lens colors during your try-on session.

**Q: What happens after I finish trying on lenses?**
A: Your try-on session simply ends once you're done browsing lens options.

## Shopkeeper Questions

**Q: Where can I find my QR code?**
A: Once your subscription is activated, you will receive a unique QR code to 
display in your shop.

**Q: How do I view my shop's analytics?**
A: You can view your analytics and customer try-on activity from your 
shopkeeper dashboard.

**Q: Can I add or remove lenses from my shop's catalogue?**
A: No, the lens catalogue is centralized and managed only by the admin.

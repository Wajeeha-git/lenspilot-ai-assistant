# Test Cases — General Company Questions

### Q1 
**Question:** What is LensPilot?
**Answer:** LensPilot is an AI-powered web platform that enables customers 
to virtually try on contact lenses using real-time iris segmentation and augmented reality.

### Q2
**Question:** What does LensPilot do?
**Answer:** It provides a virtual contact lens try-on experience for 
customers through a browser, using AI and AR technology.

### Q3 
**Question:** Who is LensPilot for?
**Answer:** LensPilot is designed for optical retailers (shopkeepers), 
and used by their customers and managed by admins.

### Q4
**Question:** What is LensPilot's mission?
**Answer:** To simplify and modernize contact lens shopping by providing 
an accurate, realistic, and accessible virtual try-on experience.

### Q5 (Easy)
**Question:** Is LensPilot a mobile app or a website?
**Answer:** LensPilot is a browser-based web platform — no app installation required.

### Q6 
**Question:** What problem does LensPilot solve?
**Answer:** It solves the problem of not being able to try on contact 
lenses realistically before buying, by offering a virtual, accurate try-on experience.

### Q7
**Question:** How does LensPilot work in simple terms?
**Answer:** A customer scans a shop's QR code, allows camera access, and 
the AI detects their iris to overlay contact lens colors in real time.

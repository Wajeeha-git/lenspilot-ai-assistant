# Test Cases — Customer Questions

---

**Q1: How do I start trying on lenses?**
Scan the QR code displayed at the shop to open the virtual try-on.

---

**Q2: Do I need to create an account to try on lenses?**
No, customers do not need an account to use the virtual try-on.

---

**Q3: Why do I need to allow camera access?**
Camera access is required so the AI can detect your iris and accurately place 
the lens overlay in real time.

---

**Q4: Can I try more than one lens color?**
Yes, you can change lens colors during your try-on session.

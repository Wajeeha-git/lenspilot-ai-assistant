# Test Cases — Product & Service Questions

**Q1: What does LensPilot's virtual try-on feature do?**
It lets customers see how different contact lens colors look on them in real 
time, using AI-powered iris detection and AR lens overlay.

**Q2: How do I access the virtual try-on?**
Scan the QR code displayed in the shop to open the virtual try-on.

**Q3: Do I need to download anything to use the try-on feature?**
No, it works directly in the browser without installing an app.

**Q4: Can I try on multiple lens colors?**
Yes, customers can change lens colors during the try-on session.

**Q5: Why does the try-on need camera access?**
Camera access is required so the AI can detect your iris and accurately place 
the lens overlay in real time.
